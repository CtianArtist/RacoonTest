Privacy information

Raccoon Supply Co. is a fictional storefront used for support-bot testing. Please do not enter real passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal details.

Demo orders and accounts

There is no real checkout, payment collection, customer account, password storage, or order history on this site. Products and order policies are illustrative.

Local contact form and demo chat

The demo contact form shows a confirmation in your browser. It does not send your message to an inbox or save it on a server. The built-in “night desk” chat answers from local help articles in your browser and does not save a conversation. Please use sample information only.

Zendesk widget

This site loads a Zendesk support widget on every page. Loading the widget may send technical information such as your IP address and browser details to Zendesk, and the widget may use cookies under its own privacy practices. If you use it, Zendesk may also receive your message. The Zendesk widget is separate from the local demo chat and contact form. Avoid sharing sensitive information while testing.

Hosting and fonts

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive standard request information when you visit. This demo does not add a separate analytics service.

About this notice

This page describes the current test site. It is not a privacy policy for a real store and should be reviewed before adding real accounts, purchases, or support operations.
