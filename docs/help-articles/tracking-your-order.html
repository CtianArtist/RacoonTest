How do I track my order?

Category: Shipping & delivery

A tracking link appears in the dispatch email when your parcel leaves our bench.

When your order is dispatched, the dispatch email includes a fictional carrier link and tracking number. It can take up to 24 hours for the first scan to appear.

No scan yet?

If the link says “label created,” the parcel is still waiting for its first carrier scan. Give it one business day. If there is still no movement after 48 hours, contact the support team with your order number.

For this demo storefront, tracking numbers are illustrative and are not connected to a live carrier.
