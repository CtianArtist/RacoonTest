Can I change my address before dispatch?

Category: Orders

Sometimes. Contact us quickly and we will try to catch the order before it is packed.

For a support-flow test, use the Zendesk widget with a sample order number and address. An address could only be updated while a real order was still unfulfilled; this demo has no real orders to change.

Once the dispatch email has been sent, the parcel is already in the fictional carrier flow and we cannot guarantee a change. In that case, contact us anyway; we will explain the available options.

Please do not include payment details in a support message.

Demo notice: This is fictional policy content. The storefront does not process real orders.
