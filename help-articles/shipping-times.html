When will my order arrive?

Category: Shipping & delivery

Most orders leave our little packing bench within 1–3 business days. Here is what happens after that.

We pack demo orders Monday through Friday from our fictional workshop in Portland, Oregon. Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3–6 business days after dispatch. During a seasonal release or a processing delay, packing can take a little longer.

The short version

  • Order received: immediate confirmation in the demo storefront.
  • Packing: usually 1–3 business days.
  • Transit: usually 3–6 business days in the fictional contiguous U.S.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Weather, carrier volume, and the occasional raccoon-related desk distraction can move the timeline. Your tracking page is the best source once a parcel is on its way.

Demo notice: This is fictional policy content. No real orders or shipments are processed.
