Account help and sign-in questions

Category: Account & contact

This demo does not create accounts, retain passwords, or offer a sign-in flow.

Raccoon Supply Co. is a frontend-only testing environment. There is no customer account, password reset, saved cart, or order history behind the storefront.

If you are testing a support flow, use the contact page to describe the scenario. Never enter a real password, payment card, or other sensitive information.
