Why is my order taking longer to process?

Category: Orders

Small-batch packing can run a little behind during launches, restocks, and rainy weeks.

Our fictional packing bench usually works through orders in 1–3 business days. Processing can stretch to 5 business days during a new collection, a restock, or an unexpected supply hiccup.

If your order has been in “processing” longer than 5 business days, contact us with your order number. We will confirm its status and provide the next useful step instead of asking you to wait without context.
