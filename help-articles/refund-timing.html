When will my refund arrive?

Category: Returns & refunds

Approved refunds are marked within 3 business days after a return is received.

After the return reaches our fictional workshop, we inspect it and mark the refund within 3 business days. Your payment provider may then take another 5–10 business days to show the credit.

This storefront is a safe content demo: no payment is collected and no real refund can be issued here. A support reply can explain the fictional policy or help test a bot flow.
